# Welcome to the Popcorn Guineapig Detekt Rule documentation! 👋

![Popcorn Guineapig Logo](./assets/img/popcorn_guineapig.png)

The goal of this Detekt plugin is to enforce architectural rules within your Kotlin project. Define your architecture guidelines through configuration, and the plugin will automatically analyze your codebase to verify compliance, helping you maintain a consistent and well-structured architecture as your project evolves.

Take a look at our [repository](https://github.com/CodandoTV/popcornguineapig-detekt-rule).
